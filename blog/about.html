About WittyFare Agrovet & Farms

Supporting farmers with quality agricultural products and solutions.

Who We Are

WittyFare Agrovet & Farms is an agricultural products business serving farmers, poultry keepers, livestock farmers, fish farmers and other agricultural customers in Abuja and across Nigeria.

We provide access to a range of agrovet, poultry, livestock, fish farming and general agricultural products to help farmers and agricultural businesses get the supplies they need.

What We Do

Our goal is to make it easier for customers to discover and purchase agricultural products without unnecessary stress.

From animal health products and poultry supplies to fish farming equipment and farm-related products, WittyFare is building a convenient platform for agricultural shopping.
